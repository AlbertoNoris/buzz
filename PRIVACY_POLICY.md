# Privacy Policy — Buzz Control

**Last updated:** March 26, 2026

Buzz Control ("the App") is developed by Alberto Noris. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your information.

## Summary

Buzz Control is designed with privacy at its core. **All your data stays on your device.** The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information with the developer, third parties, or external servers.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

The App collects the following information solely for the purpose of providing accurate Blood Alcohol Concentration (BAC) estimates:

### Personal Profile Data
- Biological sex
- Age
- Height and weight
- Habitual drinker status
- Driver type (normal, new driver, professional, or none)

### Session Data
- Alcoholic beverages consumed (name, volume, alcohol percentage, timestamp)
- Food consumption events (meal size and timestamp)
- Custom drink definitions you create

### Historical Data
- Daily summaries of past sessions (drinks, food events, peak BAC reached)
- Recovery actions and self-reported feelings

**All of this data is stored locally on your device** using the operating system's standard local storage. None of it is transmitted to any server, cloud service, or third party.

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

The App does **not** collect, store, or transmit:
- Your name, email address, or any account credentials
- Location or GPS data
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs
- Usage analytics or behavioral tracking data
- Crash reports or diagnostics
- Health data from Apple HealthKit or any other health platform
- Photos, contacts, calendar data, or any other personal files

## 3. Third-Party Services

The App does **not** integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. There is no Firebase, Google Analytics, Crashlytics, or equivalent.

The App contains links to external websites (e.g., scientific references, a feature-request board, and this repository). These links open in your device's browser; the App does not send any of your data to these websites.

## 4. Permissions

The App may request the following device permissions:

- **Notifications:** To send you local reminders (e.g., hydration or BAC-related alerts). These notifications are generated entirely on your device and do not involve any external push notification service.

The App does **not** request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, calendar, or photo library.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored on your device using iOS's standard local storage mechanisms (SharedPreferences/UserDefaults). The data is protected by your device's built-in security features, including encryption at rest when a passcode is enabled.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data remains on your device until you choose to delete it. You can remove your data at any time by:

- Deleting individual entries within the App
- Clearing the App's storage through your device's Settings
- Uninstalling the App (this permanently deletes all associated data)

Since no data is stored on external servers, deletion from your device is complete and irreversible.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. The App enforces a minimum age of 18 during onboarding.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the revised version will be posted here with an updated "Last updated" date. Since the App does not collect your contact information, we cannot notify you directly — we encourage you to review this page periodically.
